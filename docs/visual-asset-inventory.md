## Integration

- `godot/scripts/main.gd` instances GLB scenes for player, pursuer, train, low/high obstacles, coin, power-ups, and environment kits.
- OBJ and primitive paths remain as fallbacks; gameplay lane, z-threshold, jump, slide, scoring, chase-gap, and chapter rules were preserved.
- Player animation state selects `idle`, `run`, `jump`, and `slide`; the pursuer selects `idle` and `run`.
- Coin and power-up spin uses the GLB-facing Z axis.
- `SM_Track_Kit.glb` and `SM_Platform_Kit.glb` instantiate per 36-unit segment; `SM_Station_Kit.glb` and `SM_Props_RailwayKit.glb` instantiate every other segment to reduce node count.
- The chase camera was reframed to `Vector3(0.0, 4.8, 9.0)` with FOV `66.0`, fog, sun shadows, and fill light.
- The HUD now uses code-generated mobile-first styling: stat chips, a polished chase-gap meter, case-file story panel, and readable score/coins/distance/power labels.
- Collision remains code-based; authored GLB bounds informed scale alignment, but gameplay thresholds were not changed.
- There is still no external `.theme`, saved `.tres` material library, `.gdshader`, or separate texture folder; GLB textures and materials are embedded in the generated GLBs.
